Getting started

What is ATL Thrift Guide?

A curated guide to Atlanta's independent thrift and antique stores. Browse vetted stores, plan efficient shopping routes, see what other thrifters are finding, and (for Insiders) get curated local markets, swaps, and estate sales.

Do I need an account?

Without an account, you can see nearby stores and our curated routes. Create an account to browse every store, build your own routes, join the community, and submit events.

Who can use the app?

You must be 16 or older and agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Account and sign-in

I didn't get my sign-up code.

Codes come from ATL Thrift Guide <info@atlthriftguide.com> and usually arrive within a minute. Check your Spam, Junk, and Promotions folders, and make sure you typed your email correctly. On the code screen, tap Send a new code (wait a minute between requests). Codes expire after 10 minutes, so use the newest one.

It says my email already has an account.

That email is already registered. Tap Sign in instead. If you don't remember your password, use Forgot password?

That username is taken.

Usernames are unique and use 3 to 20 letters, numbers, periods, or underscores. The sign-up form shows whether a name is available as you type.

I forgot my password, or want to change it.

On the Sign in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and follow the link we send you to set a new password. Passwords need at least 8 characters, including a number and a special character.

Can I change my username or email?

You can update your profile photo, bio, and favorite category from Profile → Edit profile. To change your username or email address, contact us from the email on your account.

How do I sign out?

Go to Profile and tap Sign out.

Membership and billing

What's the difference between Member and Insider?

ATL Guide Members (free with an account) can browse every store, build routes, join the community, and submit events. ATL Guide Insiders also get curated Atlanta events and can save their favorite stores and routes for later.

How much is Insider?

$7 per month or $60 per year, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. The exact price in your currency is shown in the App Store before you confirm.

How does the free trial work?

Your trial gives you full Insider access. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it automatically becomes a paid membership, billed by Apple.

How do I cancel?

Insider memberships bought in the iPhone app are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose ATL Thrift Guide, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Insider access until the end of your current billing period. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.

Can I get a refund?

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my saved stores and routes if my membership ends?

You can still see what you saved, but saving new stores and routes requires an active Insider membership.

I subscribed, but I don't see Insider features.

Make sure you're signed in to the same ATL Thrift Guide account you used when subscribing, then close and reopen the app. If it still doesn't work, email us with your account email and the date you subscribed.

Stores and the guide

How are stores chosen?

Every store in the guide is reviewed by our team. We focus on independent thrift, vintage, consignment, and antique stores around metro Atlanta.

A store's hours or details are wrong, or it closed.

Open the store's page and tap Report an issue with this store. Choose what's wrong and add any details. Store hours and inventory change often, so we always recommend calling ahead before a special trip.

Can I suggest a store that isn't listed?

Yes! On the Discover page, tap Know a store we're missing? and fill in the short form. Our team checks every suggestion before adding it to the guide.

I own a store or run events. Can I be featured?

We'd love to hear from you. Email us about your store or event.

Routes, maps, and location

How do routes work?

Choose your starting point, what you're hunting for, and how much time you have, and we'll plan an efficient order of stops with estimated travel times. You can open the route in Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions, or share it.

Can I end my route somewhere specific?

Yes. In the route builder, under Have an end point in mind?, start typing any address or place and pick it from the list.

The app can't find my location.

Make sure location access is allowed for ATL Thrift Guide in your device settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services). You can also pick a starting neighborhood manually; the app works fine without location.

Is my location stored?

No. Your location is used on your device to suggest a starting point and nearby stores. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Events

Who can see events?

Curated events are an ATL Guide Insider feature. Anyone with an account can submit an event.

How do I submit an event?

Tap Submit an event in the Events section. All fields are required except the flyer. An official website, ticket page, or social media post works for the event link. Let us know whether you're organizing it or just sharing something you found.

When will my event appear?

Our team reviews every submission before it's published, usually within a few days. We may edit details for clarity or decline events that don't fit the guide. We may contact you at the email you provide if we have questions.

An event's details are wrong or it was canceled.

Open the event and tap Report this event. Event details come from organizers and can change, so confirm with them before you go.

Community

How do I share a find?

Tap Share a find, add a photo, what it is, where you found it, and the price. Photos are automatically resized, and hidden location data is removed before upload.

Community guidelines

Be kind and keep it about thrifting. Don't post anything hateful, harassing, explicit, misleading, or spammy, don't post other people's photos or private information without permission, and don't advertise without our okay. Posts that break these rules may be removed, and accounts may be suspended. The full rules are in our Terms of Service.

Can I delete something I posted?

If you need a post or comment removed, email us. Deleting your account removes everything you've posted.

Safety, reporting, and blocking

How do I report a post or person?

Tap Report on any community post, choose a reason, and add details if you like. Reports are private and go directly to our team.

How do I block someone?

Tap Report on one of their posts, then tap Block. You won't see their posts or comments, and they aren't notified. To unblock someone, go to Profile → Blocked accounts.

Staying safe while thrifting

Meet any private sellers in public places, trust your instincts at estate and private sales, and never share personal or payment information in comments.

Is someone in danger?

If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911. Then report the content to us so we can act on it.

Privacy and your data

What information do you collect?

Only what we need to run the app. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what we collect and why. We never sell your information and don't show ads.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us from the address on your account, and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, saved items, and notes. It can't be undone, and it doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.

Troubleshooting

Something isn't loading or looks wrong.

Check your internet connection, then close the app completely and reopen it. Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store.

The address search doesn't show suggestions.

Type at least a few characters, like a business name and ZIP code (for example, "Target 30005"), and give it a second. If it still doesn't work, check your connection and try again.

Photos won't upload.

Make sure the app has permission to access your photos or camera in your device settings. Try a different photo; very large or unusual image formats can fail. Photos must be images (not videos).

Still stuck?

Email info@atlthriftguide.com with your account email, device, and what happened, and we'll take a look.
