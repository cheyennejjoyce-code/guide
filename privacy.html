Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to run ATL Thrift Guide, we don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains how ATL Thrift Guide ("the app," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. ATL Thrift Guide is operated by Cheyenne Joyce in Atlanta, Georgia, USA. Your use of the app is also governed by our Terms of Service. If you have questions, email info@atlthriftguide.com.

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, username, and password. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form by our database provider; we can't see them.
  • Profile information (optional): a profile photo, bio, neighborhood, and favorite category.
  • Things you post or save: community finds (item details, price, caption, and photo), comments, likes, saved finds, saved stores and routes, visited stores, store votes and follows, and private visit notes (visible only to you).
  • Event submissions: event details, an optional flyer image, how you're connected to the event, and a contact email we use only for questions about your submission.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you send about stores, posts, events, or people, and the accounts you choose to block.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support email.

Information collected automatically

  • Location (only if you allow it): when you use location features, your device's location is used on your device to suggest a starting neighborhood, show nearby stores, and build directions. We don't store your location on our servers. To show the local temperature, we send an approximate location (rounded to about a mile) to our weather provider. If you open directions in Google Maps or share a route, that link may include your starting point.
  • Basic technical information: like most apps, our hosting and database providers automatically record basic request information (such as IP address, device and browser type, and timestamps) to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • On-device preferences: the app remembers a few display settings (such as your browsing view) on your device.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Photos

When you upload a photo to a post or event, the app re-processes it into a new, smaller image before upload. This also removes hidden data stored inside photos, such as the GPS location where the photo was taken. Photos you share in posts or event listings are visible to other signed-in members.

Payments

ATL Guide Insider memberships purchased in the iPhone app are processed by Apple. We never see or store your card details. We receive information about your subscription status (for example, whether it's active and when it renews) so we can unlock Insider features.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including emailing you sign-up and password reset codes.
  • To run the app's features: store guides, routes, events, the community, and saving.
  • To review event submissions and reports, and to keep the community safe.
  • To provide Insider membership features.
  • To respond to you and provide support.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.

What other people can see

Your username, profile photo, bio, and the finds and comments you post are visible to other signed-in members. Your email address, saved items, visit notes, reports, and blocks are private. Published events are visible to Insider members; your contact email on an event submission is never shown publicly.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Supabase — account sign-in, database, and file storage
  • Vercel — website and app hosting
  • Cloudflare — domain services and sending account emails (such as sign-up codes)
  • Google — maps, store and address search, and our business email
  • Open-Meteo — local weather (receives only an approximate location)
  • Apple — App Store purchases and subscriptions

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of our users or the public, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, saved items, visit notes, reports, and blocks. Events you submitted that were published may remain in the guide as community information, but they're no longer linked to you and your contact email is removed. Copies may remain in routine system backups for a limited time before they're overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile anytime from your profile page.
  • Delete your account anytime in the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • Location: you can turn off location access in your device settings; the app still works without it.
  • Block other members, and report content or accounts, from within the app.
  • Requests: to ask for a copy of your information or for help with your data, email info@atlthriftguide.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access rules that limit each person to their own private data, and verified sign-ups. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if required by law.

Age requirement

You must be at least 16 years old to create an ATL Thrift Guide account. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. If we make significant changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
